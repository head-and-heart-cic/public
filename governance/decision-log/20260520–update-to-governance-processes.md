# Decision: Update to Governance Processes in Standing Orders
**Date:** 20/05/2026

## Decision
The Governance Processes section of the Standing Orders has been updated to clarify the authoritative locations of public and internal governance documents, and to formalise the access pathway for third‑party review.

## Summary of Changes
- Confirmed that the current Standing Orders are published publicly on the H&H GitHub repository.
- Specified that all amendments to the Standing Orders must be documented on the public repository with an explanation of the reasons for the change.
- Established the definitive public source of governance documents at:
  https://github.com/head-and-heart-cic/public/tree/main/governance
- Established the definitive internal source of governance documents at:
  https://github.com/head-and-heart-cic/internal-governance
- Introduced a process allowing third parties with a legitimate reason to request time‑limited, read‑only access to the internal governance repository.
- Confirmed that a non‑authoritative convenience copy of the Standing Orders will be maintained on the H&H website:
  https://headandheart.info/standing-orders

## Rationale
This update strengthens transparency, ensures clarity about authoritative document sources, and supports good governance practice by enabling controlled external review when appropriate. The wording “legitimate reason” is intentionally broad to allow discretion and openness while maintaining secure handling of internal governance materials.
