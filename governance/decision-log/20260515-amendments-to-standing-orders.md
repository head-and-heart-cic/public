# Decision: Amendments to Standing Orders
**Date:** 15/05/2026

## Summary

The Director has amended the Standing Orders to clarify the governance mechanism, simplify the activity‑classification structure, and refine the sustainable remuneration rules. These changes do not alter H&H’s mission or constitutional purpose; they improve operational clarity, transparency, and ease of application.

## Changes Made

### 1. Updated definition of mission‑focused activity
The definition now refers directly to the **Objects in the Articles of Association**, rather than the CIC36.  
This reflects the fact that the Articles are the authoritative reference point. The wording of the Articles was copied from CIC36, so there is no significant operational change.

### 2. Refinement of remuneration rules
- The £800 earned‑income threshold remains unchanged.  
- A **maximum cap of 150 on‑contract hours** per four‑week period has been introduced.  
- The rule that the hours that can be claimed as on-contract are limited has been clarified.  
- The growth‑phase note has been simplified to:  
  *“During the initial growth phase, only mission‑focused activities can be treated as on‑contract.”*

### 3. Minor structural and clarity improvements
Formatting inconsistencies were corrected, and wording was tightened for readability and consistency.

## Reason for the Amendments

These changes strengthen the coherence and practicality of the governance framework by:

- ensuring the Activity Log remains simple, transparent, and aligned with the CIC’s legally defined mission;  
- making the remuneration mechanism easier to apply while maintaining prudence and regulatory defensibility;  
- reinforcing the Standing Orders as a public, transparent commitment rather than a bureaucratic burden.
